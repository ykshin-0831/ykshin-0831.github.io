Project

AI Guard: validating a portfolio risk engine

AI & Business Model Laboratory, September 2025 to present

My role: team lead of six. I led the team building the prototype, designed and ran its statistical validation, and re-implemented the estimators and backtest.

The engine’s original Gaussian VaR was breached 70 times in 2,562 days against 26 expected. Filtered historical simulation (FHS) cut this to 29. On unseen 2010–15 data it breached on 1.16% of portfolio-days (95% interval 0.89% to 1.44%), and in 2023–26 the Kupiec test rejected its 1% breach rate for none of 300 random portfolios drawn from the same 33 stocks.

Product
The app tells users its loss figure will be exceeded on 1 day in 100. Under the original model that happened on 2.7 days in 100; after the switch to FHS, 1.1. The tests also led us to drop incremental revaluation, which showed no consistent speed gain for a typical six-asset portfolio.

Project

Bitcoin drawdown early warning

FBA Quant project, December 2025 to present, team of three

My role: I proposed the core signal, rebuilt the system as a tested package, and ran its out-of-sample validation and follow-up studies. Teammates built the original detector.

Out of sample the frozen rule caught 17 of 66 crash episodes and a volatility rule with the same number of alerts caught 14 (McNemar p = 0.58), so the spectral rule performed about the same as the volatility baseline.

Follow-up
Whether any warning comes before onset became the question of my sole-authored paper below.
